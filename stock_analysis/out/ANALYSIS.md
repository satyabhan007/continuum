
`metrics.csv` (5 rows x 46 flat metrics), `series.csv` (2520 per-day rows).

## Key findings

- **AAPL is the best risk-adjusted performer**: highest Sharpe (1.16) and
  total return (+61.5% at only 18.8% vol). It fully recovered its -27.7%
  2023 drawdown (peak 2023-04-11, trough 2023-10-17, recovered 2024-07-11)
  but now sits -9.9% off its 52w high with a mixed trend and 2 down days.
- **NVDA earns its return with far more pain**: similar CAGR to MSFT but
  2.7x the volatility (48.7%) and a -45.4% max drawdown that has *not yet
  recovered* (trough 2024-08-29; still -18.8% below the period peak). It is
  the only symbol with a currently bullish SMA-stack trend and the only one
  with positive skew (0.19), reflecting its huge up days (best +8.19%).
- **MSFT is the smoothest ride among the winners**: max drawdown only -9.9%
  (recovered within 41 days) and Calmar 2.43, the best in the set. Currently
  on a 3-day up streak, -5.0% off its high, MACD below signal.
- **KO behaves like the defensive asset**: lowest volatility (9.7%), lowest
  1-day VaR (-0.96%), modest +17.9%. Its worst day in two years was only
  -1.75%. Sharpe 0.49 reflects the low absolute return vs the 4% risk-free.
- **GME is the clear laggard and riskiest**: -38.8% total, 73.1% vol,
  Sharpe -0.02, -57.1% max drawdown (2023-01-06 to 2023-09-01, recovered
  briefly on 2023-12-19, then re-collapsed to -52.4% below its high).
  Currently bearish trend, 4 down days, volume fading (0.73x average).
- **Cross-sectional ranking is consistent**: return, volatility, drawdown
  depth, and VaR all tell the same story (AAPL/MSFT efficient, NVDA high-beta,
  KO low-beta, GME distressed). No metric contradicts another.
- **Correlations are all near zero** (|r| <= 0.076, e.g. AAPL|MSFT -0.029).
  This is expected: the CSVs are deterministic *synthetic* data with
  independently-seeded random walks (`sample_data.py`), not real market
  data where large-cap names typically correlate 0.5-0.8.

## Caveats

- These are synthetic sample datasets generated by `sample_data.py`; the
  numbers demonstrate the toolkit's analytics and are not investment advice
  or real market history.
- Live Stooq fetch is blocked (browser-verification page), so all analysis
  is offline from the committed CSVs.
- Metrics verified independently: total return, max drawdown, volatility,
  Sharpe, CAGR, and the AAPL|MSFT correlation were recomputed from the raw
  CSVs with a separate script and match the analyzer to 3+ decimal places.
